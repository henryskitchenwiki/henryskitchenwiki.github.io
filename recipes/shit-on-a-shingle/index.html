Hello everybody, and welcome to Henry’s Kitchen, where today we’re going to be making Henry’s chipped beef on toast, otherwise referred to as shi*t on a shingle.

Shi*t on a shingle is a uniquely American dish, which traces its roots to the U.S. military, because it’s an easy dish to make when rations are low, and it contains all four groups of the food groups.
